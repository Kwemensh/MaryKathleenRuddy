— Her Story —

Each of us has a special story to tell, and incredible gifts to share with the world.

I had my first harp lesson in January of 1996. By 2012, I was a college graduate, a Returned Peace Corps Volunteer — and a 25-year-old limping through life with debilitating fatigue.

After thousands of dollars, multiple doctors, a mysterious diagnosis of chronic fatigue syndrome, and little hope of full recovery — enter Dr. Mindy Pelz. What doctors couldn't touch with prescriptions, my body healed itself once I implemented the tools I learned from her teachings.

Today I celebrate recovery. I reach out my hands to all who are still suffering, and I share my gift of music with love.

She specializes in the use of Fasting, Food Cycling, & Hormone Literacy to support the body in its innate capacity for self-healing.

  • Lifestyle Coaching is a personalized partnership designed to help you understand and work with your body — not against it. Together we examine your daily rhythms, nutrition, fasting practices, and hormonal cycles to identify where your body is asking for support.

    Unlike traditional medical care, this is not about treating symptoms. It is about creating the conditions for your body to heal itself, naturally and powerfully.

  • Coaching is especially powerful for women who have been told "everything looks normal" but still don't feel like themselves — those navigating chronic fatigue, hormonal imbalance, weight resistance, brain fog, or unexplained inflammation.

    If you are ready to move from frustration to empowerment, and from confusion to clarity about your body, this work is for you.

  • Your coach will guide you through evidence-based protocols rooted in the teachings of Dr. Mindy Pelz — including cycle-aware fasting, food cycling, and hormone literacy.
