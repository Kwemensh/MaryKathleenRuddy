Frequently Asked Questions

Answers With Clarity

What is Lifestyle Coaching?

A personalized partnership to support sustainable changes in how you eat, fast, move, sleep, and live through the lens of hormonal health.

Who will benefit from coaching?

Women at every stage of life — from perimenopause to post-menopause, and anyone seeking more energy, clarity, and stability.

What will my coach do?

Mary Kathleen helps you understand patterns, apply each healing tool, celebrate wins, and move through plateaus with support.

How much does it cost?

Schedule a free consultation to discuss coaching options that fit your needs and budget.

Will I get results?

Results come with openness and consistency. Clients report better energy, sleep, mood stability, and healthier weight management.
